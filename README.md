# New Zealand Fly Fishing Calendar

iCalendar (.ics) file for tracking New Zealand freshwater fly fishing season dates.

## Subscribe

Subscribing keeps your calendar in sync automatically. Use this URL:

`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gerhard-xyz/fly-fishing-calendar/main/fly-fishing-nz.ics`

**Apple Calendar:** File > New Calendar Subscription, paste the URL, click Subscribe.

**Google Calendar:** Settings > Add calendar > From URL, paste the URL, click Add Calendar. Note: Google typically refreshes subscribed calendars only once every 24 hours.

**Outlook:** Add calendar > Subscribe from web, paste the URL, click Import.

Tip: swap `https://` for `webcal://` to let some apps add it in one click.

## One-time import

To import a static copy instead of subscribing, download [`fly-fishing-nz.ics`](fly-fishing-nz.ics) and open it in your calendar app, or use File > Import.

## What's included

All events are all-day and recur every year. They are marked transparent so they don't block time on your calendar.

| Event                                  | Dates          | Notes                                        |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| NZ general sports fish season          | 1 Oct – 30 Apr | The main season for most rivers and streams  |
| Fishing season opens / closes          | 1 Oct / 30 Apr | Single-day markers for the general season    |
| Backcountry & designated waters season | 1 Nov – 31 May | Many backcountry/headwater rivers open later |
| Backcountry rivers open                | 1 Nov          | Single-day marker                            |
| North Island winter fishing            | 1 May – 30 Jun | Designated waters that stay open past 30 Apr |
| Winter fishing closes                  | 30 Jun         | Single-day marker                            |
| Taupō District river season            | 1 Dec – 31 May | Rivers above the winter-fishing limits       |
| Taupō District season opens / closes   | 1 Dec / 31 May | Single-day markers                           |
| Year-round fisheries                   | 1 Jan reminder | Many lakes and lower rivers never close      |

## Important caveats

Fishing seasons are **set by the regulator and change from year to year**. Treat this calendar as a planning aid, not a legal reference.

- **Verify before you fish.** Dates and rules vary by region and by individual water. Always check the current regulations for where you are fishing.
- **Most of NZ** is managed by [Fish & Game New Zealand](https://www.fishandgame.org.nz/), which has 12 regions each publishing its own _Anglers Notice_.
- **The Taupō District** is managed separately by the [Department of Conservation](https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/central-north-island/places/taupo-trout-fishery/) and needs its own licence — a Fish & Game licence is not valid there.
- A current fishing licence is required to fish for sports fish in New Zealand.
